Privacy Policy & Data processing

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Introduction

At letstry.online, we believe your virtual fitting room experience should be entirely private, secure, and secure by design. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information in connection with the letstry.online Chrome Extension (the "Extension") and the website located at letstry.online (the "Service").

We do not sell, rent, or trade shopper portrait files, search terms, or browser history.

✦ 1. Volatile Photo Processing

To provide virtual try-on previews, the Extension requires a portrait photo. This photo is processed as follows:

  • Short-lived Local Storage: Your selfie photo is stored locally on your device via chrome.storage.local. It stays on your computer and is never saved permanently on our servers.
  • Volatile AI Processing: When you generate a try-on look, the photo is sent to our secure API endpoints solely for the duration of the blending/rendering operation. It is automatically cleared immediately after the composite preview image is returned to your browser.
  • Offline Fallback (Simulator): If our API is offline or you use the simulated Try-On feature, your photo is processed entirely client-side on your own computer using the HTML5 Canvas API. No image data is transmitted over the network.

✦ 2. Storefront Garment Scraping

The Extension contains content scripts that run on the shopping websites you browse.

  • Storefront Scrape: The content script only active-scrapes when you hover a product image and click the "👗 Try on letstry.online" button.
  • Captured Metadata: It captures the image URL, product title, and price. This is stored temporarily in local storage to configure the try-on compositor.
  • No Tracking of History: We do not log your browsing history or record the storefront pages you visit unless you explicitly choose to interact with the try-on button.

✦ 3. Google Identity & Sign-In

To secure analytics, track affiliate performance, and prevent duplicate account creation, the Extension uses Google OAuth for sign-in:

  • Requested Scopes: We request access to your basic profile (email, profile name, and picture).
  • Usage: The picture and email are displayed in your personal dashboard chip at the bottom of the extension side panel. Your unique user ID is used to safely route outbound telemetry event attributes to prevent duplicate conversions.

✦ 4. Telemetry and Outbound Clicks

When you click "Buy This Look" or generate a try-on result, the Extension sends secure telemetry events to our backend. This includes:

  • The event type (e.g. TRY_ON_CLICK, OUTBOUND_AFFILIATE_CLICK).
  • The brand domain and anonymized product category.
  • These metrics are displayed in aggregate on the administrative dashboard. No individual identity metrics or shopper portrait files are linked to these public compliance telemetry streams.

✦ 5. Data Security and Deletion

You have complete control over your virtual try-on data:

  • Delete local data: You can delete your portrait at any time by clicking "Step Back & Retry" or clearing the extension's local storage.
  • Logout: Logging out clears your active Google OAuth session token and removes your local session attributes instantly.
